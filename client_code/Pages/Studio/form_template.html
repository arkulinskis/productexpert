What the red pen checks

  • Strategy: a thin diagnosis, a goal posing as a policy, fewer than two or more than five actions, an empty “will not”, fluff words, measures that are outputs, no signals to revisit (Rumelt, Porter, Roger Martin).
  • Roadmap: dates beyond Now, bets with no evidence or no problem, measures that count delivery instead of outcomes.
  • Every note links its source. It’s rule-based, not AI: it catches the classic mistakes, not whether your bet is right.
