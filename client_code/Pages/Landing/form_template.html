How you learn here

Reading about strategy doesn’t make you good at it. Every module makes you recall, judge and decide.

  1. Read a short briefingThe idea, the evidence, what strong directors do and avoid, with sources you can check.
  2. Drill itRecall cards and “director’s call?” judgements, scheduled by spaced repetition so you keep them for months.
  3. Take the challengeA realistic leadership scenario with real numbers. Write your decision, compare it with a model answer, score yourself on the rubric.
  4. Pass the exam, get the certificateEach program ends in a recall round and a capstone case. Certificates have a public verification link.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every program, lab, drill, exam and certificate is free for now. There are no ads and we don’t sell your data. If we ever add paid extras, what you’ve earned stays yours.

Who is it for?

Product managers with a few years’ experience, senior and group PMs aiming at director, design, engineering and data leaders moving into product leadership, and founders who run product themselves.

I already know Scrum and roadmaps. Will this bore me?

No. Every basic (Scrum, Kanban, stories, specs, roadmap formats, scoring models, funnels, MVPs) is packed into one module, with the senior critique of each. The other 31 modules cover what directors are judged on: strategy and moats, trustworthy experiments, growth accounting, pricing, unit economics, org design, hiring and coaching managers, boards and CEOs, transformations, AI products, platforms and regulation.

Will this make me a director?

No course can promise a promotion, and you should be wary of any that does. What it can do is give you the judgement, vocabulary and practice that director interviews and director jobs test, and a certificate that shows you did the work. The capstones are written like the cases senior product interviews use.

How long does it take?

Each module is about an hour with drills and the challenge; each program is a few evenings. With five hours a week, all eight programs take about four months. Make a plan after you sign up and it’ll give you dates.

Where does the content come from?

From primary sources and the practitioners who shaped the field: Cagan, Torres, Rumelt, Helmer, Kohavi, Grove, Dunford and many more, plus research, shareholder letters, regulators’ texts and public reporting for the case files. Every module lists its sources and the Library links to the originals.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. It’s issued by Product Expert on its own authority, says exactly what you completed, and can be verified by anyone with the link. Treat it as evidence of work, not a licence.
